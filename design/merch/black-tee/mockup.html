Production notes

For the printer

Method

Screen print with plastisol or water-based ink. DTG also works with the 300 dpi PNG files. Print a white underbase under every colour so the greens and yellow stay bright on black.

Garment

Black crew neck, 100% ringspun cotton, 180 to 200 gsm. Sizes S to XXL. Use the 10 in back print for S and M, 11 in for L and up.

Logo rules

The leaf mark prints in leaf green on black. Approved alternates are dark green, black and cream versions for other garment colours. Don't stretch, outline or add effects to the mark or wordmark.

Fine detail

The smallest elements are the front-chest wordmark (about 0.2 in cap height) and the 0.7 in leaf mark under the collar. Check both hold on the screen mesh before running the order.

Typefaces

Bricolage Grotesque Bold and Medium for the wordmark and tagline, outlined in the files.

Approval

Send a photo of one strike-off per placement for sign-off before the full run.

Files

Print-ready files

Kept in the SmileAgrimarket repository under design/merch/black-tee/. Artwork has transparent backgrounds; the black areas are the garment, not ink.
