Production notes

For the printer

Method

Screen print with plastisol or water-based ink. DTG also works with the 300 dpi PNG files. Print a white underbase under every colour so the greens and yellow stay bright on black.

Garment

Black crew neck, 100% ringspun cotton, 180 to 200 gsm. Sizes S to XXL. Use the 10 in back print for S and M, 11 in for L and up.

Logo rules

Keep the two leaves in their two greens and the seed dot in Harvest yellow. Don't recolour, stretch, outline or add effects to the mark or wordmark.

Fine detail

The thinnest elements are the 0.03 in rules on the back and the domain text under the collar. Check both hold on the screen mesh before running the order.

Typefaces

Bricolage Grotesque Bold and Medium for the wordmark and tagline; JetBrains Mono for labels and the domain. Both are outlined in the files.

Approval

Send a photo of one strike-off per placement for sign-off before the full run.

Files

Print-ready files

Kept in the SmileAgrimarket repository under design/merch/black-tee/. Artwork has transparent backgrounds; the black areas are the garment, not ink.
