SMILEAGRIMARKET - BLACK PULLOVER HOODIE

Uses the same print files as the black tee (design/merch/black-tee/):
  - front-left-chest  3.75 x 0.90 in  - wearer's left chest, print centre 4 in from centre front,
                                         top edge about 6 in below the high point shoulder (clear of the drawcords)
  - back-full         11.00 x 2.74 in - centred, top edge about 1.5 in below where the hood lies (about 7.5 in below the collar seam on size L)
The under-collar leaf mark is left off the hoodie because the hood covers that area.
Inks: white + leaf green #78C752 (nearest Pantone 360 C), white underbase.
